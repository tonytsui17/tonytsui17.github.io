Kenpax website
Kenpax is a medical supply company based in Suzhou, providing high-quality disposable medical products to clients around Asia.
Preview demo

Kenpax
year
2020
deliverables
Web design
Front-end implementation

Hong Kong

overview
The project focused on creating a new website to introduce Kenpax and its products with a more innovative and visually distinctive approach. Rather than following the conventional structure of a medical corporate website, I explored a more immersive browsing experience built around horizontal scrolling, bold visual layouts, and interactive motion.
/1
visual direction
innovative and visually engaging
As client wanted the new website to feel more innovative and visually engaging, I explored a visual direction that moved away from the typical clinical and information-heavy style of medical websites. Strong typography, large-scale imagery and bold compositions were used to create a more confident and contemporary visual identity.
horizontal scrolling
To create a more distinctive experience, I introduced horizontal scrolling as the core interaction model of the website. While scrolling, the page will move horizontally through the company's story and product portfolio. This allowed the layout to become more expressive, giving products more visual impact while creating a stronger sense of progression and discovery.
/2
webflow implementation
I brought the concept from visual design to a live website through Webflow, implementing the layouts, horizontal scrolling, responsive behavior, interactions, and animations directly during development.

Working across both design and implementation allowed me to experiment with the experience in the browser and continuously refine the interactions, ensuring the final website stayed true to the original creative direction across different screen sizes
